paradise scale, as text
========================

All figures at the working scale of 70 units = 1 metre. One cell = 4096 units = 58.5 m.


THE NUMBER THAT CHANGED
-----------------------
    your original      r = 60 units     ->  1.7 m across    a closet
    scaled to default  r = 4096 units   ->  117 m across    a small valley

    the whole original idea was 34x smaller than ONE CELL. r=60 is a door-prompt
    radius, not an environment. It works as the trigger that fires the load door.


THE LADDER, AND WHY EACH STEP IS OR IS NOT ENOUGH
--------------------------------------------------
    r (units)  across   metres   what fits
    --------   ------   ------   ------------------------------------------
          60     120     1.7 m   a door prompt. not a place.
         700   1,400    20.0 m   a yard. no Vertibird, no storm reading.
       2,048   4,096    58.5 m   exactly one cell. a room, tight.
       4,096   8,192   117.0 m   VALLEY. Vertibird fits, weather crosses, works.
       8,192  16,384   234.0 m   approaching the loaded edge. no headroom left.

    no "You cannot go that way" message.

THE ONE THING THAT DOES NOT WORK
--------------------------------
    Z-shift straight down into a "subterranean interior". It stays in the same
    CELL, and a cell is one streaming unit with one lighting set, one navmesh
    and one weather state. A separate interior with its own water table and its
    own ambient sound is unreachable that way, at any distance.

    It has to be a load door. That is why the bunker mod needed an elevator, blast
    doors and a breaker switch: those are the cost of a real instance change.


UNIT SCALE IS STILL UNVERIFIED
------------------------------
    the source document said 64 units = 1 m. the CK wiki says 4096 units = 58.5 m,
    which is 70. a 9.4% spread, and every dimension above is in units.

    we use 70. to settle it: measure a known-length NIF. a Vertibird is a known
    real-world length, so its scale in the CK gives the actual conversion.

    at 64 instead of 70, the valley above becomes 128 m across. nothing structural
    changes, but every real-world number quoted is 9% optimistic.
