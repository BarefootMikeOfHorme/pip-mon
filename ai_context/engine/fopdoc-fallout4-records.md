### Flags

Flags have contextual meaning depending on the record type. The known meanings are given below.

Flag

Meaning

0x00000001

The plugin is a master file.

0x00000200

The plugin is a light master.

### Compressed Data

Compressed data has the following format.

uint8\[ dataSize - 4 \]
